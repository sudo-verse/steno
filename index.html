General Instructions

Read before you begin. The timer starts the moment you click I am ready to begin.
  1. The total duration is 120 minutes. The countdown at the top right is authoritative; when it reaches zero the paper is submitted automatically.
  2. The paper has 200 questions across 3 sections. You may move between sections freely at any time using the section tabs.
  3. Each correct answer earns +1 mark. Each wrong answer costs 0.25 mark. Unanswered questions score zero — so leave a question rather than guess blindly.
  4. Save & Next records your answer and moves on. Clear Response removes your answer for the current question. Mark for Review flags a question to return to; a marked question is not evaluated differently — if it holds an answer, that answer still counts.
  5. Click any number in the question palette on the right to jump straight to that question.
  6. Your progress is saved in this browser as you go, so an accidental refresh will not lose your paper.
  7. This paper is invigilated. When you click I am ready to begin your browser will ask to use your camera — the paper does not start without it. Your camera stays on throughout in the background (video preview is hidden during the test to avoid distraction and can be toggled anytime), and the invigilator can watch live. Nothing is uploaded to this site.
